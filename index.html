Why this campaign

We found the weak link.

The rover’s challenge has not simply been getting it to move. Unreliable cells and voltage sag have made testing harder to interpret and repeat.

01 / POWER

Cells under load

Earlier packs built from low-cost cells showed voltage sag. The goal is to use graded, measured cells and record each pack’s performance.

02 / BUILD QUALITY

Better pack construction

Direct soldering can heat cells. The plan is to use suitable interconnects, insulation, protection and a repeatable assembly process.

03 / CONTINUITY

Keep experiments moving

Basic measurement tools and a small stock of spares can reduce downtime when electronics or wiring components fail.
